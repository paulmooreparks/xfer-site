# XFER0305

**Error.** Temporal denotes no valid instant.

## What it means

The temporal is well formed but names no real date or time: a 13th month, a 30th of February, an hour of 24, an offset of `+24:00`.

## An example

From the conformance suite, `invalid/305-invalid-time`:

```xfer
25:00:00
```

```text
$ xfer validate example.xfer
example.xfer:1:1: error XFER0305: temporal denotes no valid instant
```

## How to fix it

Correct the field that is out of range. February has 29 days only in leap years, and second 60 is allowed, as a leap second.

## In the specification

Section 4.6, [Temporals](/page/spec/lexical-structure#46-temporals), of the [specification](/page/spec) at `v1.0.0-draft.1`.
