# XFER0701

**Error.** Invalid UTF-8.

## What it means

The document is not valid UTF-8. XferLang text is always UTF-8; a file saved in another encoding, or bytes cut in the middle of a character, are refused rather than guessed at.

## How to fix it

Save the file as UTF-8. Most editors offer this in their save or encoding menu. A leading byte order mark is fine and is ignored.

## In the specification

Section 3.1, [Encoding](https://github.com/paulmooreparks/xferlang/blob/v1.0.0-draft.1/spec/xferlang.md#31-encoding), of the specification at `v1.0.0-draft.1`.
